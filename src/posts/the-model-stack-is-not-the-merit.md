Tip: don't judge yourself, or anyone else, by the model stack on the résumé. The tools are table stakes.

The real merit is what you can actually build, verify, deploy, and sustain with them. Next time you weigh a stack, a hire, or your own progress, ask the harder question: what has this shipped, and does it still hold up in production?
