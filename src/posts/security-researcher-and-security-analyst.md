If I could choose a job, I would choose to be both a **Security Researcher and Security Analyst**, combining the responsibilities of these two roles into one unified approach to cybersecurity.

As a self-taught developer with experience in Linux, backend systems, automation, and defensive security, I've always been fascinated by understanding how technology works beneath the surface.

Rather than treating security research and security analysis as two separate professions, I would combine their methods, knowledge, and objectives into a single workflow.

## Combining Two Roles Into One Purpose

**As a Security Researcher**, I would investigate vulnerabilities, explore attack techniques, study software architecture, identify weaknesses, and research how systems could potentially be compromised.

**As a Security Analyst**, I would analyze threats, investigate suspicious activity, monitor system behavior, evaluate risks, and develop practical defensive strategies.

By combining these roles, I could approach cybersecurity from both offensive and defensive perspectives, always within authorized environments.

My goal would be to create a continuous process:

**Research → Investigate → Analyze → Understand → Protect → Improve → Repeat.**

Instead of simply identifying a vulnerability and moving on, I would investigate its root cause, understand how it could be exploited, evaluate its potential impact, develop appropriate defenses, and verify that those defenses work.

This would allow me to connect theoretical research with practical security engineering.

## Why This Approach Fits Me

I'm naturally curious, analytical, and interested in understanding complex systems. I enjoy experimenting, questioning assumptions, identifying patterns, solving problems, and continuously learning.

I don't want to understand only one part of a system. I want to understand the entire picture, from its architecture and internal mechanisms to its vulnerabilities, behavior, and defenses.

My philosophy is simple:

**The deeper you understand a system, the better you can identify its weaknesses, anticipate potential threats, and strengthen its security.**

I believe cybersecurity should be more than reacting to attacks or discovering vulnerabilities. It should be about continuously improving technology, preventing problems before they occur, and protecting the people who depend on these systems.

## My Ultimate Goal

I wouldn't want to limit myself to a single traditional job description. I would develop a multidisciplinary role that combines **security research, threat analysis, vulnerability assessment, incident investigation, automation, and defensive engineering**.

One purpose, two perspectives: understanding how systems can be attacked and knowing how to defend them.

**Think like a researcher. Investigate like an analyst. Build like an engineer. Protect like a defender.**

Research to understand. Analyze to discover. Defend to protect. Improve to evolve.
