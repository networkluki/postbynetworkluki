[Read the Policy](https://readthepolicy.theinfo.nu/) summarises the privacy policy and terms of service of a website. Enter a domain and you get a concise overview of the agreements that govern your use of it.

The idea came to me at work. Privacy policies and terms of service are long, dense and written in legal language. Most people accept them without reading, even though these documents determine how their data is handled and which rights they agree to.

I wanted to lower that barrier. The goal is that anyone can understand the fine print of any website in a few minutes, without legal training and without reading pages of clauses.

What appeals to me is the practical value: a small tool that turns information most people skip into something they can actually read and act on.

Fine print → Summary → Informed choice.

For me, Read the Policy is about transparency: making it easier to know what you agree to before you click accept.
