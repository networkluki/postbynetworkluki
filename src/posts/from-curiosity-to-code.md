I'm Luki Hackwell (@networkluki), a 29-year-old self-taught developer passionate about Linux, backend development, automation, cybersecurity, and AI.

My journey began years ago after watching a documentary about programming. What started as curiosity about web development grew into a passion for understanding, building, and securing complex systems.

Today, I combine my technical knowledge and experience with AI to develop projects, solve problems, and explore new possibilities. I see AI as a powerful work tool that enhances human creativity and productivity, not a replacement for knowledge or critical thinking.

My philosophy is simple:

Your knowledge + Your experience + AI = Greater potential.

Idea → AI discussion → Self-review → Testing → Working project.

Humans steer. AI accelerates. Humans verify.
