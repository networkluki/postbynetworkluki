My world: You can be anything you want. If you don’t believe you can, you can always switch to a new world.
Life itself is like an academic education. We are always learning.
Everyone stands on the same level. Everyone is intelligent, and everyone is unique in their own way.
Those who have great resources also have great opportunities to improve other people’s lives.
I don’t need to prove my knowledge to people who have already decided to underestimate me. I observe their actions, learn from their behavior, and choose for myself who gets to be part of my life.
